Social media activity alone does not grow a business. For SMEs in Mombasa, Nairobi, and across the coast, digital marketing needs to connect visibility with enquiries, appointments, and sales.

## Define the outcome first

Before posting content, decide what success looks like:

- More phone calls or WhatsApp enquiries?
- Website form submissions?
- Walk-ins to a physical location?
- Brand awareness in a specific industry?

Your content, calls to action, and ad spend should all point to that outcome.

## Consistency beats virality

Most local businesses benefit more from steady, professional presence than from chasing viral trends. A clear content calendar — product highlights, client outcomes, team expertise, and industry tips — builds trust over time.

## Use the channels your customers already use

In Kenya, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and Google Search remain primary discovery channels for many industries. Meet customers where they are, and make it easy to contact you in one tap.

## Measure what matters

Track enquiries, not just impressions. Review which posts, ads, and landing pages generate conversations. Adjust monthly based on data, not assumptions.
